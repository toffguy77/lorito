## Why

Verb conjugation is the single most common pain point of learners of Spanish,
and Lorito teaches the rules of every tense (A1 present through C1 subjunctive
nuances) but never drills the forms. Today a learner reads "Presente:
правильные глаголы -ar -er -ir" and then has to open another app to practise
`hablo / hablas / habla`. A conjugation trainer that is driven by the same
spaced-repetition engine and unlocks tenses as their grammar cards are studied
turns Lorito's grammar into automatic production — and closes the main gap the
product audit found against dedicated conjugation apps.

## What Changes

- **New content type: verbs and tenses.** `content/conjugation/tenses.json`
  maps each drillable tense/mood to the grammar card that teaches it;
  `content/conjugation/verbs/<infinitive>.json` holds frequency rank,
  Russian translation, conjugation model, and explicit forms for every
  irregular tense. Regular forms are generated by the pipeline.
- **Pipeline:** `tools/conjugate.py` (stdlib) generates regular paradigms;
  `tools/validate.py` checks tenses, verbs, models and irregular tables;
  `tools/compile.py` writes complete paradigms into `content.json`.
- **Domain:** a pure `ConjugationPlanner` selects due/new conjugation items
  (unit = verb × tense) within the unlocked scope; a pure
  `ConjugationChecker` grades typed forms with an accent-aware rule.
- **SRS:** conjugation items reuse the `srs-engine` scheduling operation and
  the existing review store under namespaced ids (`conj:<verb>:<tense>`) as
  defined by `learning-platform-foundation`; no new persisted record type. One
  synced settings field is added (`dailyConjugationCount`), released per
  `cloudkit-schema-release`.
- **Opt-in and capped:** new conjugation items are off (quota 0) until the
  learner enables them (then 3/day by default, within the combined daily
  new-introduction budget); due conjugation reviews are capped at 20/day.
- **Free for all learners.**
- **Unlocking:** a tense becomes drillable once its grammar card has been
  studied at least once and its level is included in the learner's scope.
- **UI:** a «Спряжения» entry on Today (own daily quota) and in the catalog
  card reader of each tense card («Тренировать спряжения»); a drill screen in
  focus mode with typed answers and feedback.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `conjugation-trainer`: conjugation item model, unlocking, daily quota,
  answer checking, drill flow and its use of the SRS.

### Modified Capabilities
- `content-model`: adds the conjugation content files (tenses registry and
  verb files) and their schema.
- `content-pipeline`: adds paradigm generation, conjugation validation and
  compilation of paradigms into the bundle.

## Impact

- **Content**: new `content/conjugation/`; `content/schema.md` gains a
  conjugation section. Authoring task: tenses registry (≈ 14 tense/mood
  entries) and the first 100 verbs by frequency.
- **tools/**: new `conjugate.py`; `validate.py`, `compile.py`,
  `test_pipeline.py` extended.
- **Domain**: `Conjugation` model types, `ConjugationPlanner`,
  `ConjugationChecker`; `Card` unchanged.
- **Content (Swift)**: `ContentLoader` decodes paradigms into the catalog.
- **Features**: Today entry, drill screen, catalog entry point.
- **Persistence**: no new record type; one optional, defaulted settings field
  mapped in `SwiftDataUserDataStore` and deployed per `cloudkit-schema-release`.
- **Dependencies**: `learning-platform-foundation` (item kinds, card-only
  counting, combined new-item budget, learning day, first-review-per-day rule),
  `cloudkit-schema-release` (settings field), archived `plumaje-design-system`,
  `recall-first-study` (focus mode, interval captions). The person set per
  variant comes from `regional-variants`. Coordinates with sibling `fsrs-scheduler`
  (uses whatever scheduling operation `srs-engine` exposes), `spanish-audio`
  (optional: speak the correct form), `regional-variants` (voseo forms),
  `product-analytics` (drill events).
