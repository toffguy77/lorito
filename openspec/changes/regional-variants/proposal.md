## Why

Lorito teaches peninsular Spanish only, while a large part of its most
motivated audience — Russian speakers who moved to Argentina, Uruguay, Mexico
and elsewhere in Latin America — hears and needs a different standard every
day: `ustedes` instead of `vosotros`, `vos sos` instead of `tú eres`, `carro` or
`auto` instead of `coche`. Today a learner in Buenos Aires drills `vosotros`
forms nobody around them uses. Letting the variant drive cards, exercises,
drills and audio makes Lorito match the learner's real life — a differentiator
the audit listed among the killer features.

## What Changes

- **Extends the `spanishVariant` setting owned by `spanish-audio`**
  («Испания», «Латинская Америка») with «Риоплатский» and gives the setting
  content behaviour; no second setting is introduced.
- **Asked at the right moment:** after the learner's first completed session
  a one-time Today prompt offers the choice; it is always available in
  Settings. Onboarding is unchanged.
- **Content markup for variants:** variant blocks in card bodies
  (`:::variant am rio` … `:::`) and inline alternatives
  `{es: coche | am: carro | rio: auto}` — a syntax that does not collide with
  Obsidian wiki-links (`[[…]]`) used in the source vault. The learner's form is
  shown in place; others appear as labelled notes and in the «Варианты»
  context-menu action of `learning-platform-foundation`.
- **Exercises follow the variant:** optional per-variant answer overrides and
  a variant restriction (e.g. `vosotros` drills only for Spain).
- **Conjugation person sets:** Latin America drops `vosotros`; Rioplatense uses
  `vos` instead of `tú` and drops `vosotros`, with `vos` forms generated by the
  pipeline and overridable.
- **Voice:** Rioplatense prefers an `es-AR` voice and falls back to `es-MX`, then
  `es-ES`.
- **Free for all learners.**

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `regional-variants`: Rioplatense value of the variant setting, the variant
  prompt, rendering of variant content, exercise and drill behaviour per
  variant, voice fallback.

### Modified Capabilities
- `content-model`: card variant markup, exercise variant fields, `vos` forms.
- `content-pipeline`: validation of variant markup, voseo paradigm generation,
  compilation of variant data.

## Impact

- **Content**: markup added to cards where usage differs (second person
  plural, voseo-relevant cards, lexical differences); `content/schema.md`.
  Authoring: audit all 159 cards; add overrides to affected exercises; `vos`
  overrides for irregular verbs.
- **tools/**: validate/compile/test extended; voseo generation in
  `tools/conjugate.py`.
- **Domain**: `SpanishVariant` gains `rioplatense`; variant-aware card body
  parsing (parser in `Domain` per `learning-platform-foundation`); exercise
  answer resolution per variant; conjugation person set per variant.
- **Features**: Today prompt, settings value, variant notes in card rendering,
  «Варианты» context-menu content, voice fallback in `spanish-audio`.
- **Persistence**: no new field — the existing synced `spanishVariant` value
  gains a case; older app versions must tolerate an unknown value per
  `cloudkit-schema-release`; a «variant prompt shown» flag is added as an
  optional/defaulted settings field.
- **Dependencies**: `spanish-audio` (owner of `spanishVariant`, voices),
  `learning-platform-foundation` (parser in Domain, Spanish-span context menu),
  `cloudkit-schema-release`, `conjugation-trainer` (paradigms, person-set input),
  `listening-and-pronunciation` (recognition locale), archived
  `plumaje-design-system`. `placement-test-and-first-session` owns onboarding.
