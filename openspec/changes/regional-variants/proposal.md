## Why

Lorito teaches peninsular Spanish only, while a large part of its most
motivated audience — Russian speakers who moved to Argentina, Uruguay, Mexico
and elsewhere in Latin America — hears and needs a different standard every
day: `ustedes` instead of `vosotros`, `vos sos` instead of `tú eres`, `carro` or
`auto` instead of `coche`. Today a learner in Buenos Aires drills `vosotros`
forms nobody around them uses. Letting the learner choose their variant makes
cards, exercises, drills and audio match their real life — a differentiator the
audit listed among the killer features.

## What Changes

- **Variant choice:** Spain (default), Latin America (general) or Rioplatense,
  chosen in onboarding and changeable in settings.
- **Content markup for variants:** variant blocks inside card bodies and
  inline lexical alternatives (`coche` / `carro` / `auto`); the app shows the
  learner's form first and the others as labelled notes.
- **Exercises follow the variant:** optional per-variant answer overrides and
  a variant restriction (e.g. `vosotros` drills only for Spain).
- **Conjugation drills:** Latin American variants skip `vosotros`;
  Rioplatense adds the `vos` person, generated by the pipeline with explicit
  overrides.
- **Audio follows the variant:** Spain → `es-ES` voice; Latin America →
  `es-MX`; Rioplatense → `es-AR` when the device has such a voice, otherwise
  `es-MX`.
- **Pipeline:** validates the markup and compiles variant data.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `regional-variants`: variant setting, rendering of variant content, exercise
  and drill behaviour per variant, voice selection.

### Modified Capabilities
- `content-model`: card variant markup and exercise variant fields.
- `content-pipeline`: validation of variant markup, voseo paradigm generation,
  compilation of variant data.

## Impact

- **Content**: markup added to cards where usage differs (second person
  plural, voseo-relevant cards, lexical differences); `content/schema.md`.
  Authoring: audit all 159 cards for variant notes; add variant overrides to
  affected exercises.
- **tools/**: validate/compile/test extended; voseo generation in
  `tools/conjugate.py`.
- **Domain**: `Variant` enum in `UserSettings`; variant-aware card body
  parsing; exercise answer resolution per variant; conjugation person set per
  variant.
- **Features**: onboarding step, settings, variant notes in card rendering,
  voice selection passed to `spanish-audio`.
- **Persistence**: one new settings field (CloudKit development schema change,
  production deployment before release).
- **Dependencies**: `conjugation-trainer` (paradigms, drill persons),
  `spanish-audio` (voice by language code), `listening-and-pronunciation`
  (recognition locale follows the variant), archived `plumaje-design-system`.
